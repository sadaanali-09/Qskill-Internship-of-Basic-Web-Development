A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know
before you begin.

Some practical notes about this RazorPay demonstration.

Can I use this site to accept a real payment?+

No. RazorPay here is a frontend prototype. It does not connect to a payment processor, collect payment details or move money.

Are the product metrics, customer names and quotes real?+

No. Dashboard figures, logos, testimonials and examples are fictional placeholders made for the interface demonstration.

How can I explore the account flow?+

Create a demo account from the sign-up page to preview the sample dashboard. Demo credentials are stored in this browser only; do not use a real password.

Does RazorPay offer live integrations or pricing?+

No. Integration tiles and plan descriptions are illustrative product concepts, not offers or statements of live availability.
